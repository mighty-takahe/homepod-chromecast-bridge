Virtual Chromecast Audio Endpoints

Each HomePod Mini is advertised via mDNS (_googlecast._tcp.local., model Chromecast Audio, capability ca=199428) with a hardware-signed Eureka Gen1 ICA CastV2 TLS listener (773 windows through Dec 2030) and Eureka/DIAL HTTP server.

Why aren't speakers appearing in my phone/browser Cast menu yet? (Local Wi-Fi / LAN Required)

Google Cast discovery (_googlecast._tcp.local. on UDP 224.0.0.251:5353) and Apple AirPlay (_airplay._tcp.local.) are link-local multicast protocols that cannot cross the internet from this remote preview container into your home Wi-Fi. To see your HomePod Minis inside YouTube Music, Spotify, Chrome, or Android apps on your phone, deploy this repository on your Home Assistant server (Add-on Store) or a machine on your home LAN with --network host.

Apple HomeKit AirPlay Permissions

  • 1. Allow Speaker & TV Access: In the Apple Home app on iOS/macOS, open Home Settings → Speakers & TV and set access to Everyone or Anyone on the Same Network with no password required.
  • 2. Host Networking: When running in Docker or Home Assistant OS, keep host_network: true enabled so mDNS (UDP 5353), CastV2 TLS (TCP 8009+), and AirPlay RAOP traffic can traverse the local subnet.
  • 3. Stereo Pairs: If two HomePod Minis are joined as a stereo pair in Apple Home, pair or target the primary channel's IP address—AirPlay routes stereo L/R balance automatically.
